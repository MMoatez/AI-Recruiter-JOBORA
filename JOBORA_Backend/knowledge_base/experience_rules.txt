EXPERIENCE WRITING RULES
Write three to five concise bullet points per relevant experience when enough source facts exist. Begin with an action verb, describe the task or contribution, then include tools and a result only when explicitly supplied. Keep each bullet under 180 characters when practical. Do not manufacture metrics, responsibilities, clients, team sizes, or business impact.

Example input: "Stage Flutter 3 mois, développement application mobile, Firebase, Git."
Example style: "Développé des fonctionnalités mobiles avec Flutter." "Intégré les services Firebase." "Géré le code source avec Git."
